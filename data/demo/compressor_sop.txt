COMPRESSOR C-101 OPERATING AND MAINTENANCE SOP

Equipment ID: C-101
Equipment Type: Industrial Compressor

Normal operating temperature:
75 C to 85 C.

If operating temperature exceeds 85 C:
1. Notify the maintenance supervisor.
2. Inspect the compressor cooling system.
3. Check lubrication condition.
4. Inspect bearings for abnormal wear.
5. Do not return the equipment to normal operation until the abnormal condition is evaluated.

Visible oil leakage around the compressor housing should be reported for maintenance inspection.

Repeated temperature excursions may indicate a developing mechanical or lubrication problem.
